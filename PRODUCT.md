# PRODUCT.md

## Who this is for

Recruiters and hiring managers evaluating Gustavo Bispo for Senior Frontend or Product Engineer roles. Senior engineers and design partners are the secondary audience.

## What this is

A single-page portfolio whose interface is the proof. It should make frontend engineering visible through real layout behavior, responsive composition, interaction states, accessibility, typography, and implementation restraint. Resume facts support identification; they are not the page's content.

## What it is not

- Not a software landing page.
- Not a product demo, dashboard, fake browser, terminal, or IDE.
- Not a project gallery or career timeline.
- Not a case-study archive.
- Not a public copy of the resume.

## Public-safe content boundary

The page may use Gustavo's name, Senior Frontend & Product Engineer title, 6+ years of experience, public GitHub and LinkedIn profiles, email, broad capabilities, technology names, and selected employer names from the supplied resume as factual work history.

Do not publish employer-specific architecture, internal tools, customer or platform scale, operational processes, confidential metrics, project details, customer or client names, or implementation narratives. Employer names may establish trajectory; the resume owns the detail.

## Voice

- Direct, precise, and human.
- Confident without sales language.
- Product-aware without sounding like a product pitch.
- Technical without developer cosplay.
- Short enough to understand in under 30 seconds.

## Top-of-page promise

Within eight seconds a visitor should understand:

1. Gustavo is a Senior Frontend & Product Engineer.
2. He works across UX judgment, frontend systems, and production delivery.
3. The page itself demonstrates frontend craft.
4. Email, LinkedIn, and GitHub are immediately available.

## Content pillars

- Identity: name, role, experience level, contact.
- Frontend craft: semantic structure, responsive behavior, interaction, accessibility, and performance.
- Product judgment: framing, tradeoffs, clarity, and feedback.
- Engineering leadership: systems, quality, collaboration, and delivery.

## Behaviour

- One page, English only.
- Fully responsive and keyboard accessible.
- One signature interaction rooted in browser-native behavior.
- Reduced-motion parity.
- No theme toggle, modal, carousel, contact form, or scroll hijacking.

## Success criteria

- A recruiter identifies the role before interpreting the art direction.
- An engineer recognizes real frontend decisions rather than decorative code motifs.
- The page cannot reasonably be mistaken for a SaaS marketing site.
- No confidential or employer-specific material reaches the rendered page.
- Build, typecheck, accessibility, and responsive checks pass.
