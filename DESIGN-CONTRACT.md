# Design contract

Status: active

## Audience and job

Recruiters should identify Gustavo as a Senior Frontend & Product Engineer within eight seconds. Hiring managers and engineers should recognize product judgement, responsive systems thinking and production quality from the interface itself.

The site earns a short verdict, then a conversation. The resume owns detailed career history.

## Product form

- Single page, English only.
- Technical-editorial composition, not a software landing page.
- One browser-native signature interaction: a range input that changes the real width and container-query state of an editorial specimen.
- Selected employer names and broad roles may establish trajectory.
- Email, LinkedIn and GitHub remain immediately available.

## First viewport

The hero must show:

1. Gustavo Bispo.
2. Senior Frontend & Product Engineer.
3. Six or more years.
4. React, TypeScript and Next.js.
5. Direct contact links.

The measured desktop composition must survive common widths from 1280 to 1600 pixels. Mobile recomposes to four columns without horizontal overflow.

The live width ruler belongs to the responsive specimen section, where the control and its result remain visible together.

## Visual rules

- Warm paper, near-black ink and one cobalt signal.
- Magra for display, Inter Tight for prose and JetBrains Mono for measurements.
- Flat surfaces, square edges, structural hairlines and authored rhythm.
- The measurement grid is permitted only in the hero and live specimen.
- No rounded stage, cards, glass, shadows, blobs or ornamental illustrations.
- No fake browser, IDE, terminal, dashboard or code window.
- No logo wall, skills cloud, proficiency chart, testimonials or project gallery.

## Privacy boundary

Never render employer-specific architecture, tools, internal products, processes, customers, scale, metrics, decisions or project narratives. The old case-study routes must resolve to the homepage and must not import case-study content into their route bundles.

## Interaction and accessibility

- Native range semantics and keyboard arrows are preserved.
- The range value, output and controlled specimen width must agree.
- Mobile controls may not claim a width larger than the physical surface being demonstrated.
- Visible focus, skip navigation, semantic heading order and reduced-motion parity are required.
- Primary contact targets must remain operable by touch and keyboard.

## Verification

- Production build and TypeScript complete successfully.
- ESLint reports no errors in app, src and scripts.
- Axe reports zero WCAG A/AA violations.
- Smoke tests pass for the homepage, redirects and metadata routes.
- Screenshots are inspected at 1440 by 1000 and 390 by 844.
- The first viewport passes the Impeccable comp gate at 65% or better.
